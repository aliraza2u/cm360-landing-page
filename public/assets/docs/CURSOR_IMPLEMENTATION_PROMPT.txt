- vivid but professional blue/indigo/cyan color system
- strong dark/gradient hero
- large layered CM360 product screenshots
- asymmetric sections
- alternating white / soft-blue section rhythm
- controlled glows, depth and blueprint/construction cues
- fewer repetitive bordered cards
- product UI is the main visual
- attractive first viewport that immediately communicates construction + records + money + workforce management
