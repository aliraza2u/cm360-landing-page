When you confirm a deletion request, the request remains pending for a **14-day cancellation period**. You can continue using CM360 during that period and can cancel the pending request from My Profile while it remains pending.

When an Owner’s company-and-account request is finalized, the company workspace is closed and company files and operational data enter CM360’s deletion process. Remaining operational company data becomes eligible for permanent purge **7 days after closure**. Limited audit, billing, anonymized, or legally required records may be retained as described in the [Privacy Policy](https://cm360.site/privacy).
